labelcraft

A free label designer that runs in your browser and prints to NIIMBOT label printers (D110, D11, B1, B21 and more) over Bluetooth or a USB cable. Place text, shapes, QR codes, barcodes and images anywhere on the label, pick an official label size, add one of 67 frames, and print. No install, no account.

Printing needs Chrome, Edge or Opera on a computer, or Chrome on Android. labelcraft needs JavaScript to run.

Independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by NIIMBOT.
